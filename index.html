💧 Liquidity

Short-Term Liquidity & Working Capital

Assess the firm's ability to cover short-term obligations without relying on external financing.

Current Ratio

Liquidity
$$CR = \dfrac{Current\ Assets}{Current\ Liabilities}$$

Short-term debt coverage. Target $\ge 1.5\times$ to $2.0\times$.

Quick Ratio (Acid-Test)

Liquidity
$$QR = \dfrac{Cash + Sec. + Receivables}{Current\ Liabilities}$$

Stricter test excluding inventory. Target $\ge 1.0\times$.

Cash Ratio

Liquidity
$$Cash\ Ratio = \dfrac{Cash\ \&\ Equiv.}{Current\ Liabilities}$$

Most conservative liquidity metric. Target $\ge 0.2\times$.

🛡 Solvency

Long-Term Solvency & Financial Leverage

Evaluate long-term debt structure, capital stability, and interest coverage capability.

Debt-to-Asset Ratio

Solvency
$$D/A = \dfrac{Total\ Liabilities}{Total\ Assets} \times 100\%$$

Assets financed by creditors. Target $\le 40\%$.

Times Interest Earned (TIE)

Solvency
$$TIE = \dfrac{EBIT}{Interest\ Expense}$$

Operating profit vs. interest cost. Target $\ge 3.0\times$.

⚡ Activity

Operational Efficiency & Asset Turnover

Measure how effectively assets and working capital are deployed to generate revenue.

Inventory Turnover

Activity
$$IT = \dfrac{COGS}{Average\ Inventory}$$

How rapidly inventory is sold and replaced per year.

Receivables Turnover

Activity
$$ART = \dfrac{Net\ Credit\ Sales}{Average\ Receivables}$$

Credit collection speed. Days outstanding $= 365 \div ART$.

Total Asset Turnover

Activity
$$TAT = \dfrac{Net\ Sales}{Total\ Assets}$$

Sales generated per dollar of total assets owned.

📈 Profitability

Earnings Performance & Return Metrics

Examine management's efficiency in generating returns for shareholders and stakeholders.

Net Profit Margin

Profitability
$$NPM = \dfrac{Net\ Income}{Net\ Sales} \times 100\%$$

Percentage of revenue remaining after all expenses.

Return on Assets (ROA)

Profitability
$$ROA = \dfrac{Net\ Income}{Total\ Assets} \times 100\%$$

Profit earned per dollar of total assets. Target $\ge 5\%$.

Return on Equity (ROE)

Profitability
$$ROE = \dfrac{Net\ Income}{Shareholders'\ Equity} \times 100\%$$

Return on equity capital. Target $\ge 15\%$.

Earnings Per Share (EPS)

Profitability
$$EPS = \dfrac{Net\ Income - Preferred\ Div.}{Weighted\ Avg.\ Shares}$$

Net profit allocated to each share of common stock.

Price-to-Earnings (P/E)

Profitability
$$P/E = \dfrac{Market\ Share\ Price}{EPS}$$

Valuation multiple of share price relative to annual EPS.

💵 Cash Flow

Cash Flow Dynamics & NOPAT

Analyze operating cash generation and free cash flow.

NOPAT

Cash Flow
$$NOPAT = EBIT \times (1 - Tax\ Rate)$$

Net Operating Profit After Tax.

Operating Cash Flow Ratio

Cash Flow
$$OCF\ Ratio = \dfrac{Cash\ Flow}{Current\ Liabilities}$$

Ability to service short-term debt from operations.

Free Cash Flow (FCF)

Cash Flow
$$FCF = OCF - Capital\ Expenditures$$

Cash available for distribution or debt repayment.

🎯 Budget

Compound Growth & Financial Projections

Forecast future value and investment maturation over time.

Future Value (Compound Growth)

Budget
$$FV = PV \times (1 + r)^n$$

Project future value at annual rate $r$ over $n$ years.
