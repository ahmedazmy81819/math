Liquidity Ratios

How well can the company meet short-term obligations?

Current Ratio

Liquidity
$$CR = \dfrac{Current\ Assets}{Current\ Liabilities}$$

Core liquidity test. Ratio ≥ 2.0 = healthy buffer. Below 1.0 = risk.

Quick Ratio (Acid Test)

Liquidity
$$QR = \dfrac{Current\ Assets - Inventory}{Current\ Liabilities}$$

Stricter: excludes inventory. Target ≥ 1.0.

Cash Ratio

Liquidity
$$Cash\ Ratio = \dfrac{Cash + Equivalents}{Current\ Liabilities}$$

Most conservative. Only counts cash. Target ≥ 0.5.

Solvency Ratios

Long-term financial stability and debt coverage

Debt-to-Asset Ratio

Solvency
$$D/A = \dfrac{Total\ Liabilities}{Total\ Assets} \times 100$$

% of assets financed by debt. Target ≤ 40%.

Times Interest Earned

Solvency
$$TIE = \dfrac{EBIT}{Interest\ Expense}$$

Operating profit vs. interest cost. Target ≥ 3.0×.

Activity / Efficiency Ratios

How effectively are assets being used?

Inventory Turnover

Activity
$$IT = \dfrac{COGS}{Average\ Inventory}$$

Times inventory is sold per year. Higher = leaner ops.

Receivables Turnover

Activity
$$ART = \dfrac{Net\ Credit\ Sales}{Average\ Receivables}$$

Collection speed. Days Outstanding = 365 ÷ ART.

Asset Turnover

Activity
$$AT = \dfrac{Net\ Sales}{Average\ Total\ Assets}$$

Revenue per dollar of assets. Higher = more efficient.

Profitability Ratios

Is the company generating adequate returns?

Net Profit Margin

Profitability
$$NPM = \dfrac{Net\ Income}{Net\ Sales} \times 100$$

Cents kept per dollar of revenue. Sector-dependent.

Return on Assets (ROA)

Profitability
$$ROA = \dfrac{Net\ Income}{Avg.\ Total\ Assets} \times 100$$

Profit extracted from every asset dollar. Target ≥ 5%.

Return on Equity (ROE)

Profitability
$$ROE = \dfrac{Net\ Income}{Avg.\ Equity} \times 100$$

Shareholder return. Target ≥ 15% for most industries.

Earnings Per Share (EPS)

Profitability
$$EPS = \dfrac{Net\ Income - Preferred\ Div.}{Weighted\ Avg.\ Shares}$$

Profit attributable to each common share. Higher = better.

Price-to-Earnings (P/E)

Profitability
$$P/E = \dfrac{Market\ Price\ Per\ Share}{EPS}$$

Market premium over earnings. Lower can mean undervalued.

Cash Flow Analysis

Actual cash generated and available to the business

NOPAT

Cash Flow
$$NOPAT = EBIT \times (1 - T)$$

Net Operating Profit After Tax — baseline for OCF.

Operating Cash Flow (OCF)

Cash Flow
$$OCF = EBIT \times (1-T) + Depreciation$$

Cash from core operations. Positive = healthy engine.

Free Cash Flow (FCF)

Cash Flow
$$FCF = OCF - NFAI - NCAI$$

Cash left after CapEx and working capital needs.

Cash Budget

Plan cash inflows, outflows, and net position

Cash Position

Budget
$$Ending = Beginning + Receipts - Disbursements$$

Positive ending balance = surplus. Negative = shortage requiring financing.
